# NutriTrack — AI-Powered Nutrition Tracking 🥗📸

**NutriTrack** is a premium, AI-powered food tracking application that revolutionizes how you log your meals. Instead of manually searching through databases for every ingredient, NutriTrack lets you simply snap a photo of your food. Powered by Google Gemini AI, it instantly analyzes the image to estimate calories, macros, and provide a health score for your meal.

Designed with a stunning dark-mode glassmorphic interface, NutriTrack makes health tracking not just easy, but beautiful.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 📸 Snap & Track (AI Food Recognition)
- **Instant Analysis**: Take a photo or upload an image of your meal.
- **Gemini AI Engine**: Our backend uses Google's advanced Generative AI to identify the food items in the image.
- **Nutritional Breakdown**: Automatically estimates total calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats.
- **Nutrition Score**: Gives your meal a health rating (1-100) based on macronutrient balance and food quality.

### 📊 Dashboard & Daily Tracking
- **Neon Calorie Ring**: A premium, glowing circular progress ring that tracks your daily calorie intake against your personalized goals.
- **Macro Progress Bars**: Track your Protein, Carbs, and Fat intake with color-coded, gradient-filled progress bars.
- **Recent Meals**: View your daily food diary at a glance in frosted glass cards.

### 📈 Analytics & Trends
- **Calorie Charts**: Visualize your daily calorie intake over 7, 14, or 30 days with beautiful bar charts.
- **Score Trends**: Track how your eating habits are improving over time with the Nutrition Score line chart.
- **Most Eaten Foods**: Discover your dietary patterns and most frequently consumed meals.

### 👤 Personalized Experience
- **Custom Goals**: Set your daily calorie limit and customize your macronutrient targets.
- **Dietary Preferences**: Set your diet type (e.g., Vegan, Keto, Paleo) to improve the AI's contextual understanding.
- **Theme Support**: Seamlessly switch between the premium Dark Mode and a clean Light Mode.

### 🔒 Secure Authentication
- **Dual Login**: Support for both Email/Password authentication and one-tap Google OAuth login.
- **Cloud Sync**: Powered by Supabase, your data is securely synced to the cloud and accessible anywhere.

## 🎨 Design & UI

NutriTrack was built with a major focus on aesthetics and user experience:
- **Glassmorphism**: Extensive use of blurred, frosted glass containers for a deep, layered look.
- **Glowing Accents**: Neon glows and gradients make key data points (like the calorie ring and buttons) pop off the screen.
- **Fluid Animations**: Smooth transitions, scaling hover effects, and animated charts provide a premium feel.

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Sign Up**: Create an account using email or Google.
2. **Set Goals**: Input your age, height, weight, and daily calorie target.
3. **Log a Meal**: Tap the glowing `Scan` button in the navigation dock.
4. **Take a Photo**: Capture your meal.
5. **Review**: The AI analyzes the photo and returns the nutritional breakdown in seconds.
6. **Save**: The meal is added to your daily diary and updates your dashboard progress!
